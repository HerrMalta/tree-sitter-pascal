================================================================================
Automatic Semicolon Insertion (ASI) Tests
================================================================================

These tests verify that tree-sitter correctly inserts automatic_semicolon
tokens when semicolons are missing but can be recovered.

================================================================================
Missing semicolon in var declaration
================================================================================

var
  a: Integer
  b: String;

---

(root
  (codeFragment
    (declVars
      (kVar)
      (declVar
        (identifier)
        (type
          (typeref
            (identifier)))
        (automatic_semicolon))
      (declVar
        (identifier)
        (type
          (typeref
            (kString)))))))
